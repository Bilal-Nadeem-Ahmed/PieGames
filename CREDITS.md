# Credits

All artwork in Pie Games is drawn in code. Music and most sound effects are generated in the app.
The background songs are traditional public-domain nursery tunes ("Twinkle Twinkle", "Mary Had a Little Lamb",
"Old MacDonald", "Row Your Boat") plus an original tune.
