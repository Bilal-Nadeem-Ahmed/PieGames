# Pie Games 🎈🐑

A collection of simple, ad-free games for toddlers, packaged as an Android app (APK) to sideload.
Everything works offline. Art is drawn in code and music/effects are generated; animal sounds are free recordings listed in [CREDITS.md](CREDITS.md).

## Games
- **Pie Pet** – a modern Tamagotchi: each child picks a puppy, kitten, bunny, lamb or dino that lives in its own room. Its needs (🍎 food, 😊 fun, ⚡ energy, 🛁 clean, ❤️ health) change in real time, even when the app is closed. Feed it (drag food to its mouth), stroke it, play (ball, teddy, bubbles, music), bath it (scrub with the sponge), switch off the light for bed, sweep up its messes, and give medicine when it's poorly. Good care makes it grow: Baby → Little → Big → Grown-up. Grown-ups → **New pet for …** lets a child start again.
- **Balloons** – endless popping in a meadow. Buttons at the top choose what's inside: 🎈 count the pops, 🎨 colours, 🐮 animals (real animal sounds), ABC letters, 123 numbers. Farm animals (sheep, cow, duck, dog, cat, chicken, horse, elephant) float down from surprise balloons. Tapping her cartoon plays the family "balloons" voice note.
- **Bubbles** – a quiet underwater bubble-pop (no kids, no talking): lots of fish to tap, a crab, jellyfish, coral; surprise bubbles release fish.
- **Run & Jump** – the selected child runs through changing places (meadow → farm → seaside → snow → sunset). Tap to hop, hold to jump higher, double-jump. Low stars can be grabbed running, high stars need a jump. Bumping into sheep/elephants/dinos/logs makes them fall over, see stars and get back up (sometimes with the family "standing" voice note).
- **Dino Eggs** – tap the patterned eggs (spots, stripes, zigzags, stars) to crack them; they wiggle by themselves to ask for a tap, and one tap before hatching a baby eye peeps through the crack. Four kinds hatch (T-rex, long-neck, triceratops, stegosaurus) wearing their shell as a hat. Tap a baby for a squeaky roar and a jump, tap the pterodactyl when it flies past. When the whole nest has hatched, mummy dinosaur walks in, roars, and the babies dance then follow her off...
- **Colouring** – tap-to-fill, freehand brush, rainbow colour, ⭐ stickers, eraser, undo. Includes colouring pages of each kid, a ladybird and 8 animals (puppy, kitten, bunny, lamb, cow, elephant, duck, dinosaur). Work saves automatically.
- **Music** – choose 🥁 drums (kick, snare, toms, hi-hat, cymbals), 🎸 guitar (tap or strum the strings), 🎹 piano (multi-touch, slide for glissando), 🎼 xylophone (rainbow bars, tap or slide) or 🔔 handbells (swing and ring). Every part lights up or moves when played; sounds are synthesised live. Background music pauses while playing.
- **Peekaboo** – the drawn farm animals (and sometimes the kids) hide behind bushes, barn doors, haystacks, boxes, trees, theatre curtains and flowerpots. Ears peep out now and then as a hint; tap to find them, then tap again for a hop and love-eyes.
- **Shapes** – 🔷 drag shapes into matching holes on a wooden board, or 🎨 put fruit and toys into the basket of the same colour.
- **Build it** – pick a house, car or rocket: saw a plank, then hammer the nails and screw the screws as each part flies in; it gets painted and the house puffs smoke (builder waves from the door), the car drives off, the rocket blasts off.
- **Feed** – up to 4 animals (puppy, kitten, bunny, lamb, cow, elephant, duck, dino) wander around the farm; each hungry one shows the food it wants in a bubble. Drag food to it: it stops, opens its mouth, munches and does a happy wiggle with its own sound. Stroke an animal to pet it (love eyes and hearts), tap it to make it hop. Wrong food gets a head shake. They get hungry again after a while, and new animals wander in.
- **Pairs** – memory game with 2, 3, 4 or 6 pairs of big flip cards from 40+ pictures: drawn animals, farm animals (with their sounds), a ladybird, penguin, giraffe, unicorn, cars, trains, fruit… and the kids' own faces.
- **Sheep pen** – tap the wandering sheep to make them leap into the pen; they're counted out loud, the gate shuts, they dance, then run out for another round.
- **Puzzles** – 2, 4, 6 or 9 big pieces to drag onto a faint guide picture: sheep, dinosaur, balloons, ladybird, fish, the kids themselves, and 8 animals at the farm, seaside, snow and sunset.
- **Find it** – 🐞 ladybird hunt (ladybirds crawl around the garden and hide under leaves; tap to find them and they fly away, counted) and 🔍 find the animal ("Where is the cow?").
- **Counting** – tap and count, "how many?", "find the number", pop the number, trace the number.
- **Letters** – A-is-for-apple flashcards, "find the letter", pop the letter, trace the letter.

Screen direction: Balloons, Bubbles and Sheep pen turn the phone upright; the home screen, the grown-ups area and all other games are sideways. The app turns the screen itself.

Quick buttons (top right): talking voice on/off, and music (tap to change song or turn off). Songs: Twinkle Twinkle, Mary Had a Little Lamb, Old MacDonald, Row Your Boat, Sheepy Hop.

## Grown-ups area
On the home screen, **hold the gear button for 2 seconds**. Edit each kid's cartoon (hair, skin, clothes, bow, toy hammer…) and name (spoken in games), and change settings: sounds, talking voice, family voice notes, background music, numbers up to 5/10/20, pop speed/amount/size, counting aloud, surprises, runner speed.

## Levels
Each child has their own level in each game (the ⭐ badge at the top right shows it, the little bar shows progress to the next level). Levels go up automatically after a couple of wins, with a big "Level up!" star.
- **Start one game again:** hold the ⭐ badge for 2 seconds – that game goes back to level 1.
- **Start everything again for a child:** Grown-ups → Settings → **Levels → Start again for …**.

| Game | What changes as the level goes up |
|---|---|
| Balloons / Bubbles | a level every 20 pops: a bit faster, one more balloon, slightly smaller |
| Run & Jump | a level every 20 stars: faster, things on the path more often, more high stars |
| Counting | numbers up to 3 → 5 → 10 → 15 → 20 (never past *Numbers up to* in settings), more choices |
| Letters | 2 → 3 → 4 choices, level 4 uses lowercase letters |
| Shapes | 3 → 6 shapes; 2 → 4 colour baskets |
| Feed | 2 → 3 → 4 animals, level 4 adds a food nobody wants |
| Sheep pen | 3 → 4 → 6 → 8 sheep that wander faster |
| Find it | 3 → 8 ladybirds with more of them hiding; 3 → 6 animals to search |
| Dino eggs | 2 → 5 eggs that need more taps |
| Peekaboo | 2 → 5 hiding places |
| Puzzles / Pairs | 2 → 9 pieces / 2 → 6 pairs (the number buttons still let you pick) |

## Voices
The games speak with recorded family voices (Dad and Rahma by default). Where more than one voice has recorded a word, one is picked at random using the **How often each voice is used** sliders (Rahma mostly, by default).
- **Grown-ups → Voices**: pick a voice, then ▶ play, ● record (tap again to stop) or ⬆ upload a clip for any word. **+ Add a voice** for anyone else (e.g. Ibrahim, Grandma).
- **Remove default voices** turns off Dad & Rahma so only your own voices are used (**Bring back default voices** undoes it).
- Words nobody has recorded stay silent (or switch on **Phone voice for missing words**).
- What still needs recording: [docs/VOICE_CLIPS.md](docs/VOICE_CLIPS.md). To add a batch of clips to the app itself: name them `<words>-pie.wav` / `<words>-me.wav`, run `python3 scripts/import_voice.py <folder>` then `node scripts/voice-checklist.mjs`.

## Install on Android
1. Copy `dist/PieGames-<version>.apk` (only the latest build is kept) to the phone/tablet (or download it there).
2. Open it and allow "Install unknown apps" for your file manager/browser when asked.
3. Tip: turn on **App pinning** (Settings → Security) so little ones can't leave the app.

The talking voice uses the device's built-in text-to-speech (Google TTS). If it's silent, check Settings → Accessibility → Text-to-speech.

## Develop
```bash
npm install
npm run serve            # play in a browser at http://localhost:8080
npm run apk              # build dist/PieGames-<version>.apk (needs Android SDK + JDK 21; version is versionName in android/app/build.gradle)
```
Code lives in `www/` (plain HTML/CSS/JS modules, no bundler). Capacitor 8 wraps it for Android.
Builds are signed with `android/app/piegames-debug.keystore` so each new APK installs over the old one and keeps saved data.
